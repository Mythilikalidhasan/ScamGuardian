SCAM TYPE: Delivery / Courier Scam

DESCRIPTION:
A delivery scam uses a fake or misleading message about a parcel, shipment, delivery problem, customs issue, address problem, or payment requirement. The sender may try to make the recipient click a link, provide personal information, make a payment, or install an application.

COMMON TACTICS:
- Fake parcel or courier notifications
- Claims that a delivery cannot be completed
- Requests to update an address through a link
- Small payment requests for delivery or rescheduling
- Fake refund or failed-delivery messages
- Links to websites that imitate courier or service providers
- Requests to install unknown applications

WARNING SIGNS:
- You are not expecting a parcel
- Unexpected link or attachment
- Urgent request to pay or update information
- Website address does not match the delivery company's official website
- Request for OTP, PIN, password, or banking credentials
- Unknown customer-support contact

WHAT TO DO:
- Verify the parcel using the delivery company's official website or application.
- Do not click unexpected delivery links.
- Do not share OTP, PIN, password, or card details.
- Do not install an application sent through an unsolicited delivery message.
- If payment is requested, independently verify the order and recipient details first.
- Report suspected cyber fraud through the appropriate official reporting channel.

EXAMPLE:
"Your parcel is on hold. Pay ₹25 through this link to reschedule delivery."

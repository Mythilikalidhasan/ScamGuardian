SCAM TYPE: Bank Impersonation / Bank and KYC Scam

DESCRIPTION:
In a bank or KYC scam, fraudsters pretend to be a bank, financial institution, wallet provider, or service provider. They may claim that KYC must be updated, an account is blocked, a payment failed, or a refund is waiting. The aim may be to obtain confidential information or money.

COMMON TACTICS:
- Fake KYC update requests
- Account blocking or suspension threats
- Requests for OTP, PIN, password, CVV, card details, or login credentials
- Fake customer-support messages or phone numbers
- Suspicious verification links
- Requests to install remote-access or unknown applications
- Fake calls pretending to be bank, wallet, or telecom representatives

WARNING SIGNS:
- Urgency or fear
- Impersonation of a trusted bank or financial service
- Unexpected link
- Request for confidential information
- Request for payment or transfer
- Caller or website provides a number that is not verified through the official bank website

WHAT TO DO:
- Never share account number, login ID, password, PIN, UPI PIN, OTP, ATM/debit/credit card details, or CVV with an unsolicited caller or message.
- Do not click a KYC link received unexpectedly.
- Access the official website or official application of the bank or financial service directly.
- Do not download unknown applications.
- If you receive an OTP for a transaction you did not initiate, contact the bank or service provider immediately.
- Report unauthorized transactions immediately to the relevant bank/service provider and the appropriate cybercrime authority.

EXAMPLE:
"Your bank account will be blocked today. Complete KYC immediately using this link."

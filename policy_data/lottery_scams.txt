SCAM TYPE: Lottery / Prize / Fictitious Offer Scam

DESCRIPTION:
A lottery or prize scam tells a person that they have won money, a prize, or another benefit even though they did not knowingly enter a genuine competition. The fraudster may then request a processing fee, tax, conversion charge, clearance fee, or other payment before releasing the supposed prize.

COMMON TACTICS:
- Unexpected lottery-winning messages
- Claims of large cash prizes or foreign funds
- Fake official letters, emails, websites, or certificates
- Requests for processing, tax, conversion, or clearance fees
- Repeated requests for additional payments
- Impersonation of banks, regulators, or public authorities

WARNING SIGNS:
- You are told you won something you do not remember entering
- You must pay money before receiving the prize
- The sender asks for confidential banking information
- The message creates urgency
- Official names or logos are used to make the offer appear genuine
- More payments are requested after an initial payment

WHAT TO DO:
- Do not pay a fee to receive an unexpected prize or foreign-fund offer.
- Do not share OTP, PIN, password, or banking credentials.
- Do not trust an official-looking document without independent verification.
- Verify the organization through its genuine official website.
- Report suspected fraud to the appropriate law-enforcement or cybercrime authority.

EXAMPLE:
"Congratulations! You won ₹50,000. Pay ₹999 processing fee to receive your prize."

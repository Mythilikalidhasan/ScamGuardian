SCAM TYPE: Investment / Financial Scheme Scam

DESCRIPTION:
Investment scams use false or misleading promises about investments, returns, financial opportunities, or schemes. A scammer may promise unusually high or guaranteed returns, create urgency, or request money through an unauthorized channel.

COMMON TACTICS:
- Promises of unusually high or guaranteed returns
- Pressure to invest immediately
- Fake investment platforms, websites, apps, or social-media profiles
- Requests to transfer money to an unfamiliar account
- Claims that a payment is required to release profits or funds
- Impersonation of financial institutions or officials
- Fraudulent investment schemes presented as legitimate opportunities

WARNING SIGNS:
- Guaranteed or unrealistic returns
- Pressure to invest quickly
- Unverified company, platform, or representative
- Request for payment to unlock supposed profits
- Communication through an unexpected social-media account or message
- Lack of clear, independently verifiable information

WHAT TO DO:
- Do not invest only because of an unsolicited message or social-media recommendation.
- Verify the organization and scheme independently.
- Do not send money to an unfamiliar account because of pressure or promises of guaranteed returns.
- Be cautious about schemes claiming to be authorized by government or financial authorities.
- Preserve messages, payment information, and website details if you suspect fraud.
- Report suspected financial/cyber fraud through the appropriate official authority.

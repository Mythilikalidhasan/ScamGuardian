SCAM TYPE: Phishing

DESCRIPTION:
Phishing is a scam in which a fraudster sends a fake email, SMS, WhatsApp message, social-media message, or website link that appears to come from a trusted organization. The goal may be to make the recipient reveal confidential information, click a malicious link, or make a payment.

COMMON TACTICS:
- Fake bank, wallet, government, delivery, or service messages
- Messages containing suspicious verification or login links
- Requests to update KYC or verify an account
- Messages creating urgency or fear
- Fake notifications about account blocking, refunds, rewards, or payments
- Spoofed emails or SMS messages that imitate a trusted organization

WARNING SIGNS:
- Unexpected message or link
- Urgent demand to act immediately
- Request for OTP, PIN, password, CVV, or other confidential information
- Website address with spelling or domain differences
- Threat that an account will be blocked or suspended
- Request to download an unknown application

WHAT TO DO:
- Do not click unexpected links.
- Do not share OTP, PIN, password, CVV, or other confidential information.
- Check the organization using its official website or official application.
- Do not download an unknown application from a message or caller.
- If financial information may have been exposed, contact the relevant bank or service provider immediately.
- Report suspected cybercrime through the official Indian cybercrime reporting system.

EXAMPLE:
"Your account will be blocked today. Verify your details immediately using the link below."

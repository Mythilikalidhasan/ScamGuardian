SCAM TYPE: UPI / Digital Payment Scam

DESCRIPTION:
UPI and digital-payment scams attempt to trick a person into approving an unwanted transaction, sharing payment credentials, or interacting with a fraudulent payment request. Fraudsters may misuse UPI collect requests, fake QR codes, fake customer-support identities, or other payment-related messages.

COMMON TACTICS:
- Fake UPI collect/payment requests
- Messages asking the user to enter a UPI PIN to receive money
- Unauthorized QR codes
- Fake payment or refund messages
- Fake bank or wallet customer-support numbers
- Requests to install an unknown application for payment assistance
- Urgent requests to transfer money

WARNING SIGNS:
- Unexpected payment request
- Request to enter UPI PIN to receive money
- Unknown QR code
- Unknown or unverified customer-support number
- Urgency or pressure
- Request to install an unknown application

WHAT TO DO:
- Do not approve an unexpected UPI collect request.
- Remember that receiving money does not require entering a UPI PIN.
- Be cautious when scanning an unfamiliar QR code.
- Do not share UPI PIN, OTP, card details, or passwords.
- Use the official bank or payment application's support channel.
- If an unauthorized transaction occurs, contact the bank/payment service immediately and report the incident through the appropriate cybercrime channel.

EXAMPLE:
"Refund pending. Scan this QR code and enter your UPI PIN to receive the refund."

SCAM TYPE: Job / Work-from-Home Scam

DESCRIPTION:
A job scam uses a fake or misleading employment opportunity to obtain money or personal information from a job seeker. Scammers may promise easy work, unusually high earnings, or immediate selection and then ask for a registration fee, processing fee, training fee, deposit, or other payment.

COMMON TACTICS:
- Work-from-home offers promising unusually high earnings
- Fake recruitment messages sent through SMS, WhatsApp, email, or social media
- Claims of immediate selection without a normal recruitment process
- Requests for registration, verification, training, or processing fees
- Requests for personal or financial information before the employer is verified
- Pressure to pay quickly to secure a position

WARNING SIGNS:
- Very high income promised for little or no work
- Upfront payment required to obtain the job
- Unverified employer or recruiter
- Pressure to act immediately
- Suspicious links or forms
- Requests for sensitive banking information unrelated to a legitimate recruitment step

WHAT TO DO:
- Verify the company and vacancy using an independently found official website.
- Do not pay an upfront fee merely to obtain a job.
- Do not share OTPs, PINs, passwords, or banking credentials with a recruiter.
- Be cautious with unexpected recruitment messages and links.
- Keep copies of suspicious messages and transaction records if a scam is suspected.
- Report suspected cybercrime through the appropriate official reporting channel.

EXAMPLE:
"Work from home and earn ₹5,000 per day. Pay ₹499 registration fee to start."
